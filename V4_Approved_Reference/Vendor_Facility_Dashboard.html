How these figures are calculated

Physical units come from non-deleted Shop-backed rentable units; companion/default Stalls are not counted as extra units. Vendor assignment, valid rent contracts, expected monthly rent, billed rent, applied payments and overdue invoice balances are distinct measures. Held financial evidence must be disclosed separately. This prototype does not perform calculations.
